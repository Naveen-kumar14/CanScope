### Calculations (E-CALC)

- **LED current:** (3.3 V − V<sub>f</sub>) / 220 Ω. With V<sub>f</sub> ≈ 1.8–2.2 V that is 5.0–6.8 mA per LED, within ESP32 GPIO drive and typical 20 mA LED ratings.
- **Buzzer base drive:** I<sub>B</sub> = (3.3 − 0.75) V / 1 kΩ ≈ 2.6 mA. For I<sub>C</sub> ≤ 30 mA the forced β is ≈ 12. That saturates PN2222A / 2N3904 / BC547 / S8050 class parts (h<sub>FE</sub> well above 12 at these currents). R<sub>PD</sub> = 10 kΩ diverts 75 µA, which is negligible.
- **Termination:** 120 ‖ 120 = 60 Ω. With a third 120 Ω: 40 Ω. With 5 % parts the 60 Ω reading spans 57–63 Ω.
- **3.3 V budget per node:** ESP32 module (Wi-Fi off) plus SN65HVD230 at 17 mA max (E-TI). Node 3 adds the OLED (≈ 10–20 mA typical, unverified) and LEDs at ≤ 7 mA each. That is well within the DevKit's on-board regulator, but the regulator part is unverified.
